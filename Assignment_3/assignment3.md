# Research Sensors and Actuators

## Sensor: Grove Temperature & Humidity Sensor (DHT11)

- **What it does:** Measures ambient air temperature and humidity level.
- **Hardware inside:** A capacitive humidity sensing element and a thermistor 
  (a resistor that changes resistance based on temperature).
- **Analog or Digital:** Digital — outputs data via a single-wire serial protocol.
- **Units and Range:**
  - Temperature: 0°C to 50°C, accuracy ±2°C
  - Humidity: 20% to 90% RH, accuracy ±5% RH

## Actuator: Grove LED (Light Emitting Diode)

- **What it does:** Emits light when electrical current passes through it. 
  Used as a visual indicator (e.g., turn on when it's dark).
- **Hardware inside:** A semiconductor diode that emits photons when forward-biased.
- **Analog or Digital:** Digital — it is either ON or OFF based on a HIGH/LOW signal.
- **Units and Range:**
  - Input: 3.3V or 5V digital signal
  - Output: Light (typically red, green, or blue depending on the LED type)
